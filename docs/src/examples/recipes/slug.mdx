---
title: Slug
description: "An editable slug computed from the title with compute, Vietnamese diacritics included."
---

The slug follows the title until the user edits it. Clearing the slug resumes syncing on the next title change; a saved post keeps its custom slug ("Load saved post"). See [Computed fields](../../guide/core/computed.mdx#editable-vs-read-only-fields).
